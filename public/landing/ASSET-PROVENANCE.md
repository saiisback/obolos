# Landing asset provenance

- `fonts/GeistPixel-Circle.woff2` is the unmodified Geist Pixel Circle webfont from the Vercel Geist project. It is redistributed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 in `fonts/OFL.txt`.
- The active logo uses `/brand/obolos-symbol-black.png` and `/brand/obolos-symbol-white.png`, original Obolos artwork generated with the built-in image-generation tool. Both are transparent versions of the user-approved interlocking O symbol. The old `assets/logo.webp` is retained as a legacy asset.
- The primary BubbledotICG-FinePos display font, Inter UI font, and Font Awesome icons are loaded from the CDN URLs specified in the landing brief and are not bundled here.
