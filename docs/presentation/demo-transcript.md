## Record on the Mac without installing anything

1. Open the visual bed in QuickTime Player. Keep playback at **1×** and position it at the beginning. Put this script in a separate window outside the recording area.
2. Press **Shift–Command–5**, select **Record Selected Portion**, and frame the video content at 1280×720 or larger. Under **Options → Microphone**, choose your microphone. Record in a quiet room with no music.
3. Start screen recording, play the visual bed, and read the script in your own voice. Stop recording immediately after the final slide. Do not use a phone or generated/cloned narration.
4. In QuickTime Player, use **Edit → Trim** to remove only idle leading/trailing time, then save the recording. Keep the actual footage at original speed. Verify the final file is **2–4 minutes**, at least **720p**, and your speech is audible throughout.

Alternatively, record your own microphone using **QuickTime Player → File → New Audio Recording**, save it locally as an M4A, and tell the assistant its local path. We can align and mux that real recording with the visual bed without changing playback speed.
