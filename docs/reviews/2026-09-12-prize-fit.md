## Hedera: mandatory evidence and extra credit

The [official Hedera prize page](https://ethglobal.com/events/ethonline2026/prizes/hedera) requires a live testnet/mainnet x402 service settled through Blocky402, a consuming platform/agent completing one real paid request, public setup/architecture/payment-flow README, and a paid-execution video of at most five minutes. Obolos retains [public HTTPS purchase](../evidence/2026-09-08-public-data-purchase.md), [self-service paid workflow](../evidence/2026-09-09-self-service-testnet.md), native transfer verification and setup docs. New Arc service-category purchases do not themselves demonstrate Hedera settlement.

Per-repository metering and the discoverable directory already support extra-credit claims. A2A/ACP negotiation, ERC-8004/HCS-14 identity, UCP/directory discovery, HTS/custom fees, HCS payment audit trails, and recurring/streamed Scheduled Transactions are **extra points**, not mandatory gates. The September 12 design is a plan; new source/tests without retained paid execution do not establish completed bonus demonstrations.

Bounded implementation notes for the ongoing bonus work:

- Installed `@x402/hedera` 2.25.0 pins `@hiero-ledger/sdk` 2.85.0 and proto 2.31.0. SDK declarations expose `Transaction.schedule()`, `ScheduleCreateTransaction.setScheduledTransaction`, `setExpirationTime` and `setWaitForExpiry`. There is no need to infer missing scheduling support from absent `@hashgraph/sdk`.
- [Hedera scheduled-transaction concepts](https://docs.hedera.com/hedera/core-concepts/scheduled-transaction) describe immediate execution when sufficient signatures arrive or delayed execution with wait-for-expiry. A finite recurring workflow needs actual schedule entities and retained schedule/scheduled-transaction IDs plus transfer/delivery proof; an application timer alone is not native scheduling evidence.
- [Blocky networks/assets docs](https://blocky402.com/docs/networks/) advertise Hedera HBAR and HTS, use the token ID as `asset`, and require the advertised facilitator fee payer. [Testnet docs](https://blocky402.com/docs/testnet/) specify real testnet transactions with open access. `src/lib/integrations/hedera.ts` currently pins `asset === 0.0.0` and rejects token transfers in its mirror proof. HTS therefore needs explicit token metadata/atomic-unit mandates and token-transfer validation rather than only replacing the asset string. Hosted facilitator acceptance and token account readiness must be established by the actual bounded HTS run before claiming settlement.
